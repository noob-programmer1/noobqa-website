common questions

pairing

  • my partner's code expired. codes only live for 5 minutes. tap "generate a new code" and try again — the new one will be fresh.
  • the qr scanner won't focus. make sure the app has camera permission in Settings → crayon → Camera. you can also tap "i'll type it" and enter the 6 digits by hand.
  • i'm trying to pair but it says we're already paired. one phone = one couple. open Settings → "ask {partner} to unpair" first, or use force-end if they're not around.

the widget isn't updating

  • open the app once — iOS sometimes throttles widget refreshes until the parent app is foregrounded. one tap is usually enough.
  • check that the widget is on your home screen or lock screen (Settings → "set up widget" walks you through it again).
  • if it's been silent for more than an hour, write to us — we keep a per-device log on our backend and can tell what went wrong.

i'm not getting pokes

  • open Settings → Notifications → crayon and confirm "Allow Notifications" is on and "Time-Sensitive Notifications" is on.
  • poke is intentionally rare — your partner can only send a few per day. it's not broken, it's quiet by design.
  • if a poke from them never arrived, write to us with the approximate time and we'll trace it.

switching phones

  • open Settings → "switching phones?" on your old phone. you'll get a 6-digit transfer code valid for 20 minutes.
  • on your new phone, open the app and tap "welcome back" — enter the code.
  • your couple, days-together, and subscription move with you.

subscription, billing, restoring

  • subscriptions are managed by Apple — open Settings → manage subscription, or Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on iOS.
  • only one partner needs to subscribe. the other unlocks automatically.
  • if you bought lifetime and don't see it on a new device, tap Settings → restore purchases.
  • refund questions: reportaproblem.apple.com (we can't refund directly — only Apple can).

i want my data deleted

  • tap Settings → unpair (with your partner) — that wipes drawings, moods, pair keys, and your name from both phones and our servers.
  • for a complete account wipe (including the anonymous user row), email tech.noobqa@gmail.com with your support code and we'll do it within 14 days.
  • full details in our privacy policy.

crash, freeze, or "something feels off"

  • open Settings → "something feels off?" and tap "send to support". it opens an email with a redacted log attached.
  • if you can't open the app at all, write to us from a different account — include your phone model and iOS version.
