crayon
a crayon for two.

two crayons. one canvas. nothing else.

a tiny widget for two. draw something, they see it once, it disappears. the only couple app that doesn't want your attention.

download on the app store support

what you can leave for each other

a doodle

in your favorite crayon color. they tap, it replays once, then fades.

a soft wash of color

when words feel like too much. eight crayons. you decide what each one means.

a hidden love note

tucked under the color. they scratch it open with a finger.

a same-sky moment

when you both open the app at once, the canvas warms.

a poke

when you really need them. a tiny tap on their shoulder — capped, intentional, the only thing that ever rings.

days together

quietly on the widget. no streaks to break, no pressure to perform.

silent by design

we don't buzz you. we don't badge you. when they think of you, it lands on your home screen and softly on your lock screen — that's the whole thing.

the widget is what you peek at. the only thing that ever rings out is a poke from your one.

every drawing dissolves the moment they see it. every note fades after it's read. we don't keep your drawings. ever.

only you two — not even us.
drawings, color moods, and questions are end-to-end encrypted on your device. the server sees ciphertext, and only briefly. viewed messages are deleted within ~60 seconds. unviewed messages expire after a day. your name is whatever you typed on day one. read the privacy policy for the long form.

for long-distance loves. partners apart. new couples and old ones. anyone whose person feels far away.
